# City of Lies

A short, single-player detective game made in Unity 2019.4 as part of a group university project.

You play as a detective investigating a murder in the city. Gather clues, talk to suspects, and build your case before stepping into court.

---

## How to Play

- **Move** -- Arrow keys or WASD
- **Interact / Advance dialogue** -- Enter
- **Navigate menus** -- Mouse click

No installation needed. Open the game link in your browser and start playing straight away.

## About the Game

- **Engine:** Unity 2019.4.41f2 (LTS)
- **Platform:** WebGL (plays in browser)
- **Controls:** Keyboard and mouse only
- **Players:** Single-player
- **Estimated playtime:** 5 to 10 minutes
- **Theme:** City

The game has two investigation paths and multiple playthroughs. Each path leads to a different suspect and a different courtroom ending. Clues you collect change what the characters say and what evidence you can present.
